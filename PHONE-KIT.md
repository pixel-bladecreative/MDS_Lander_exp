# Phone Kit

You are going to be on the floor with a customer walking up, not at a desk.
These are the prompts to paste into the Claude app on your phone when you need
something in the next ninety seconds.

**Set this up once:** create a Project in the Claude app called "Mazda," and add
the four `reference/` files to its project knowledge. Then Claude already knows
the lineup, the incentives, the altitude argument, the military programs, and
the compliance rules — and these prompts get much shorter and much better.

If you have the skills installed, just say what you need in plain language and
the right one loads. These are the fallback.

---

## New lead just came in

```
New lead. Draft me a text I can send right now.

Name:
Vehicle:
Source:
What they said:
Consent on file: yes / no / unknown

Under 40 words, sounds like a person, one question, offer two specific times.
Sign it [YOUR NAME]. If consent is unknown or no, tell me to call instead.
```

## They asked what the payment would be

```
Payment scenario. Search for this month's current Mazda incentives first —
do not use anything you remember.

Vehicle/trim:
Selling price:
Down/trade:
Credit tier:
Term:
Military or first responder: yes / no

Show cash vs. APR side by side with total interest on each, and tell me which
one actually wins. Estimates only — I'll confirm with the desk.
```

## They're comparing against a RAV4 / CR-V / Telluride

```
Customer is cross-shopping [VEHICLE] against our [MAZDA].
Give me the three honest advantages and the one place we genuinely lose,
plus how to handle that one. Include the altitude argument only if it
actually helps here.
```

## They hit me with an objection

```
Customer said: "[exact words]"
What are they actually asking, and what do I say? Keep it short — I'm
standing in front of them.
```

## Walking out to shoot a video for someone

```
Shooting a 1:1 video for [NAME] about the [VEHICLE].
They asked about [THING].
Give me 5 bullets, not a script. Include one honest flaw to point out.
```

## Planning the day

```
Here's my list — build me a ranked plan. Cap at 12.
Flag anyone I should call instead of text.

[paste CRM view / lead list / service appointments]
```

## Need content ideas

```
Give me 5 video scripts I can shoot in one session at [LOCATION].
Colorado Springs local angles. Education, not inventory.
Hook, script, b-roll, caption for each.
```

## Aged lead re-approach

```
Lead from [X] days ago, went cold. Consent status: [    ].
Write the no-pitch version — I just want to know if they landed somewhere good.
```

---

## Three things to never do on your phone

1. **Never send anything without reading it first.** Every message goes out
   because you decided it should, not because a tool generated it.
2. **Never text anyone whose consent you have not confirmed.** $500–$1,500 per
   message. When unsure, call.
3. **Never post a payment figure publicly** without term, APR, down payment,
   and credit tier alongside it.

## The 20-second version

Honestly, once the Project is set up, most of the time this is enough:

> "New lead, Dana, CX-5 Preferred, came in off the website, said she needs
> something safe for her daughter. Draft the text."

Claude has the context. Talk to it like you would talk to a coworker who
already knows the store.
