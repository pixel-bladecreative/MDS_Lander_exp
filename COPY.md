## Section 1 — How it works

**Kicker** How it works
**H2** Honestly? It's three steps.

**1 · Tell us where you are**
Text us your zip, the date you have in mind, and roughly how many people you're
feeding. That really is the whole first step.

**2 · We'll build the visit**
Our team confirms your group, your drips and the window you want. Give us 48
hours so we can staff your visit properly.

**3 · We show up**
We arrive fully equipped and we're with each person about 30 to 45 minutes. You
get to stay right where you are.
